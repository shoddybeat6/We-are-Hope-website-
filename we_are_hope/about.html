About We Are Hope

We Are Hope is a non-profit organisation dedicated to improving the lives of disadvantaged communities throughout South Africa.

Our organisation focuses on helping people become more self-sufficient while providing practical support to communities that need it most.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create stronger communities where people have access to support, education and opportunities for a better future.

Our Values

Compassion

We care about the people and communities we serve.

Equality

We believe everyone deserves dignity and opportunity.

Empowerment

We help people develop the skills and resources needed to become self-sufficient.

Community

We believe positive change happens when communities work together.

We Are Hope

Working together to bring hope and support to our communities.
